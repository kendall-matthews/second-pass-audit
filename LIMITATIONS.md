# Limitations

Second-Pass Audit does not:

- prove the source is true, current, complete, or authoritative
- guarantee correctness or safety
- eliminate hallucinations
- replace primary research
- replace medical, legal, financial, technical, security, compliance, or other qualified review
- replace customer evidence
- replace executive or human judgment
- establish universal performance improvement
- require every low-risk task to receive a full audit

AI can also miss issues while conducting the audit.

The person or organization acting on the output retains accountability.
