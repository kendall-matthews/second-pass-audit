# Quick-use prompt

```text
Run a Second-Pass Audit.

Work or recommendation:
[paste or attach what I am considering accepting]

Decision-relevant source:
[paste, attach, or link the material, evidence, requirements, terms, or constraints that should govern it]

Intended use / decision:
[state what I am about to approve, present, publish, sign, buy, follow, or act on]

Compare the work directly against the source before acceptance.

Check for material omissions, factual or interpretive errors, unsupported conclusions, weak interpretations, underweighted details, missed constraints or qualifiers, changed emphasis, and downstream inconsistencies.

Preserve supported content. Do not rewrite for novelty.

Return:
1. Decision: SHIP, REVISE, or HOLD.
2. Short rationale.
3. Material issues, each with its source basis and required correction.
4. Affected downstream elements that need rechecking.
5. Next action.

Matching the source does not prove the source is right.

If the source is insufficient or consequential expert judgment remains unresolved, return HOLD.
```
